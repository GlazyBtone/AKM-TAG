AKM GRAFFITI WARS v7.4 — ROAD TRAFFIC

This update replaces the simple colored rectangles with small, detailed top-down/isometric car models: body, roof/glazing, wheels, headlights and tail lights. Four cars loop continuously along separate paths placed on visible asphalt roads: the upper-left avenue, the road along the memorial square, and the industrial street. Car overlays ignore pointer input and do not modify the original city artwork. Existing map pan/zoom, markers, landmarks, window shimmer and skaters are preserved.

Deploy the contents of this folder or upload the ZIP to Netlify.
