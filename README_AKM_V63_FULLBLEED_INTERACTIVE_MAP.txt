AKM GRAFFITI WARS v6.3 — FULL-BLEED INTERACTIVE MAP

This build uses the approved full-city composition as the map artwork, including the skatepark under the viaduct and the Sova art residency in their intended positions. The image is only the visual city layer; territory hit areas, selected boundary paths, map navigation, wall hotspots, and place actions remain separate interactive SVG/HTML layers.

Changes:
- Continuous full-screen city artwork; no side black bars or small centered postcard.
- Initial view starts closer, with map movement and zoom preserved.
- Large battle/daily mission cards are hidden while browsing the map; the player HUD is compact and floats over the scene.
- Duplicate territory marker overlays removed; transparent hit targets keep the artwork's single colored territory markers selectable.
- Territory outlines are independent SVG paths and appear when a territory is selected.

Deploy by uploading this ZIP to Netlify Drop or publishing the folder as a static site. After deployment, verify the final layout on iPhone Safari.
