AKM GRAFFITI WARS v7.3 — LIVING CITY / NO TERRITORY BOUNDARIES

This build removes all visible territory boundary geometry. Territory names and influence still appear only after selecting one of the seven colored territory markers. The existing map artwork, aspect ratio, initial zoom, pan/zoom behavior, territory placard, graffiti walls, Sova and skatepark art layers are preserved.

New ambient details:

- Selected warm/cool window lights softly vary in brightness.
- Two small skaters loop around the frozen lake.
- Ambient overlays do not modify the original city background and do not intercept map input.
- Reduced-motion preference is respected for window animation and SVG motion.
